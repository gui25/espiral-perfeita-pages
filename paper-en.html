Tension Cascade: the concept reformulated on a scientific basis

13/11/2026 · @Guilherme Bernardo Silva

Summary

The reformulated concept states that our universe is a region of a larger energy structure that divided, and that the observed expansion is the redistribution of that energy. It is a hypothesis with several candidate mechanisms, not a confirmed theory.

Of the five claims into which the concept decomposes:

  • one has moderate statistical support in the data (about 3σ);
  • one has a strong hint still under verification (4.8σ);
  • two are consistent with known physics, but currently untestable;
  • one has been ruled out inside the observable universe and only survives outside it.

Note on the term "Vortex": "Vortex" is used here in the physical sense of a phase defect of a field: a point around which the phase completes a full turn. The concept does not claim that the universe rotates. The global rotation test was performed solely for completeness and yielded a null result.

The concept in five claims

Separating the concept into distinct parts makes it possible to test each one independently without the failure of one bringing down the others.

# Claim In physical terms Status
1 A parent structure exists A larger field or medium, of which our universe is a region Consistent, untestable
2 It divides in a cascade Daughter regions arise via instability, and divide again Excluded inside horizon; open outside
3 Each region is slightly different Initial field conditions vary from region to region Consistent, untestable
4 Expansion is energy redistribution Energy transfers between dark matter and a field, altering expansion Supported at 3.2σ
5 Tension has a handedness / direction The field violates parity and rotates the polarization of light Hint at 4.8σ

Regarding claim 3: since the initial value of the field is continuous, there would be infinitely many variations, and among infinitely many regions some would be arbitrarily similar to our own.

Candidates for each claim

Each claim has more than one possible physical mechanism. None was invented here: all already exist in the literature, and the concept acts as a framework that brings them together.

Parent structure and division (claims 1 and 2)

Candidate Idea Fit with the concept Status
A. Quantized vortex splitting In a superfluid, a multiply-charged vortex is unstable and splits into unit vortices It is literally a larger vortex dividing Observed in the laboratory; the cosmic version is an analogy
B. Eternal inflation Bubbles nucleate inside an inflating vacuum; each bubble is a universe Repeated division, with different values in each bubble Searches for bubble collisions in the cosmic microwave background found no signal
C. Universes inside black holes Torsion prevents the singularity and each black hole generates a universe; parameters shift slightly each generation Closest to the concept: cascade with small variations Smolin's testable prediction (no neutron star above 2 solar masses) is under pressure: 2.08 ± 0.07 has already been measured
D. Turbulent cascade Large eddies transfer energy to smaller eddies The intuitive picture of the hierarchy Holds for fluids; there is no hierarchy of this kind on the largest scales of the universe

Current observational synthesis: The model that best combines current data is G + J: a single field that exchanges energy with dark matter and rotates light polarization. E enters as the interpretation of the initial conditions of that same field.

What has already been tested

Model G + J, with two extra parameters, fits distance data better than the standard model by Δχ² = −13.5, equivalent to 3.2σ.

Tests of vortex division

Test What the idea predicts What the data say Verdict
Vortex core inside the horizon Polarization rotation varying by at least 0.64° across the sky Upper limit of 0.033 deg², about 0.18° Excluded
Gravitational weight of the vortex String tension Gμ of at least 0.14 Upper bounds of order 10⁻⁷ (from memory) Excluded
Scale hierarchy Log-periodic oscillations in the cosmic microwave background Not observed Unsupported
Global rotation (not claimed by the concept) — (ω/H)₀ < 7.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ Null

The field that accounts for dark energy has a scale close to the Planck mass. A vortex of such a field would be gravitationally enormous, and therefore none can exist inside the observable universe.

Open anomaly that fits the concept

The distribution of quasars and radio galaxies exhibits a dipole roughly twice as large as expected, with significance between 3.3σ and 5.1σ depending on the analysis. It can be read as our region participating in a larger bulk motion. The model does not compute this effect; it is an interpretation, not a prediction.

Predictions and refutation criteria

With m and b fixed by the distance fit, model G + J makes four predictions capable of falsifying it.

Prediction Value Tested by Refuted if
Polarization rotation at reionization 0.14° (half the recombination value) Full-sky polarization missions such as LiteBIRD The ratio is close to 1 or to 0
Rotation in sources at z ≈ 1 0.08° Polarized radio-galaxy surveys Compatible with zero with error below 0.03°
Dark energy in 5-year DESI Apparent w₀ ≈ −0.84 and wa ≈ −0.52, with matter 0.315 DESI, results expected around 2027 Preference for evolution disappears, or requires much stronger evolution
Structure amplitude S₈ = 0.813 DES, KiDS, Euclid, Rubin S₈ converges to 0.79 or lower, or to 0.836

Fifth decisive test (pending): Computing the full cosmic microwave background power spectrum with coupling b = 0.062. This value lies close to the upper bound allowed for this class of models.

Claims 1, 2, and 3 currently have no testable predictions. The only conceivable trace of neighboring regions would be a minute variation of polarization rotation across the sky, far below current sensitivity.

What was removed from the original version

Element from the original article Reason for removal
The title "theorem" and "peer review" Nothing was mathematically proven as a theorem, and the review was by the author
Derivation of the Ginzburg-Landau equation from an action A conservative action does not generate dissipative terms
Closed-loop recyclable reservoir A closed system relaxes to equilibrium; data require energy flow in the opposite direction to the article
Torsion bounce, cyclic universe, and S³ geometry No observational evidence; data are compatible with a flat universe in continuous expansion
Renormalization group limit cycle The transition function was chosen by hand to evade the Planck satellite bound
A single unified mechanism for spirals across six scales Each scale has its own cause: density waves in galaxies, chemistry in DNA, rotation and wind in the solar spiral
Numerical validation Tested the code against its own formula rather than against observational data

Spirals of different shapes, directions, and dimensions remain possible as phenomena. What was removed is the claim that they all arise from the same law.

Open questions

"Micro-gravity" still lacks a definition in the model, and there are two possible readings backed by real physics.

Reading What it would mean Existing research line Status
Gravity as a residual effect of the medium Gravity is not fundamental; it emerges from the collective behavior of the parent structure Analogue gravity in superfluids, where waves propagate as in curved spacetime; emergent gravity Speculative; laboratory analogues exist, but the cosmic version has no test
Weak gravity between regions Each daughter region feels only a small residual of the tension of the larger structure None directly; the anomalous cosmic dipole would be the only related datum Unformulated

Other questions the model does not answer

  • What divides and why. Vortex splitting in the laboratory occurs because two unit vortices cost less energy than a multiply-charged one. The equivalent for the parent structure is missing.
  • Local gain and global loss. Observational data require the condensate to lose energy on the cosmic average. Forming patterns requires local gain. It has not been shown that the two coexist.
  • High initial field value. 3.4 Planck masses is theoretically uncomfortable.
  • Light-coupling coefficient. Must be roughly ten times larger than the typical value in simple models.
  • Originality. Model G + J belongs to a known class (coupled quintessence with an axion-like field). The specific contribution here would be the cascade framework, which is precisely the part still lacking a direct test.

References and method limitations

Calculations were performed in this analysis using custom numerical integration, without independent review. The sources below were consulted via search summaries rather than read in full.

Limitations

  • The cosmic microwave background entered only as two anchors (primordial matter density and distance to the last-scattering surface), not as a full angular power spectrum.
  • The fit uses χ² minimization rather than full MCMC posterior sampling.
  • Coupling effects prior to recombination were neglected.
  • Bounds cited "from memory" (cosmic string tension, upper bound on b, homogeneity scale) require full verification.
